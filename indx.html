2. Story: Greg's Trip to London

Listen to Greg talking to Tom and Kevin about his trip to London.

Tom: Welcome back, Greg! When did you arrive?

Greg: Thanks! We arrived home yesterday afternoon.

Kevin: I've never traveled abroad. How was London?

Greg: It was interesting. You see, they speak a bit differently there.

Tom: What do you mean?

Greg: Well, on the first day, we went to the tourist information office to get directions to the British Museum. The man there told us that the fastest way to go was by Tube. We didn't understand, so he explained that that's what they call the subway.

Kevin: Really? That's funny!

Greg: There's something even funnier. Have you ever heard the phrase "zebra crossing"?

Tom: No, I haven't.

Greg: It's the crosswalk! I laughed when I thought about it!

Greg: Yes, I did! I tried "fish and chips". That's fish with fries.

Question 1: Did Greg have any difficulties in London?
